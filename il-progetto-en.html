The Origin

A project of research and outreach

Tlon is a cultural and philosophical research and outreach project conceived by Maura Gancitano and Andrea Colamedici. The name comes from the imaginary planet created by Borges in his story from Ficciones: a fictional (and dangerous) world that, by being thought into existence, becomes real.

Our mission is to recover the original way of doing philosophy through dialogue and human encounter, blending different registers, connecting research and public engagement.

I

School of Philosophy

Courses, seminars, podcasts. Philosophy as a tool for living better and thinking critically about the contemporary world. The podcast "Scuola di Filosofie" is among the most listened to in Italy. "Pensare Europeo" was created in collaboration with the European Parliament, "Meno per Meno" with Niccolò Fabi on RaiPlaySound.

II

Publishing House

Edizioni Tlon was founded in February 2016. We publish philosophy (Planetari Big), gender studies (Numeri Primi), current affairs (Soglie, Urano), pamphlets (Dardi), spiritual practices (Radici), novels (Finzioni), poetry (Controcielo).

III

Bookstores and Bookshops

On October 2, 2016, the Libreria Teatro Tlon opened at Via Federico Nansen 14 in Rome's Ostiense district. Since 2021, we manage Tlon Aleph at the National Gallery of Modern and Contemporary Art and the Villa Medici bookshop. In Venice, the Libreria Giovanni at the Fondazione Querini Stampalia.

IV

Festivals and Events

Philosophical direction of the Festival del Pensare Contemporaneo in Piacenza and the Carnival of Putignano. Creators of Il Pod — Italian Podcast Awards, the Festa della Filosofia, and Prendiamola con Filosofia. We bring thought where the unexpected can happen.

V

Corporate Training

Educational programs on philosophy, AI, diversity & inclusion, emotional education, and the meaning of work. We collaborate with companies, institutions, and universities in Italy and internationally.

The Beginning

Tlon is born with a series of cultural events in Rome. The name comes from Borges' story: an imaginary planet that becomes real by being thought into existence. And supplants reality with brutality and sweetness.
