Events we create and direct

Piacenza

Festival del Pensare Contemporaneo

Since 2023, the city of Piacenza has been working on the conception and realization of a Festival of national scope and international appeal with which, year after year, to inspire a future more aware of what we can be and achieve together. A space, both physical and intellectual, to stimulate dialogue and cooperation between personalities and audiences diverse in age, interests, histories and origins.

Philosophical direction by Tlon · Curation by Alessandro Fusacchia

02 Milan · Teatro Carcano

Lezioni di Meraviglia

Learning to wonder, to not take the world for granted, to exercise the erotic dimension of complexity. A series of encounters where Andrea and Maura dialogue with musicians, scientists and artists to address what seems unapproachable: the apparent meaninglessness of existence.

03 Rome · Libreria Teatro Tlon

Literary Parties

Evenings celebrating the love of books, inspired by the phenomenon born in New York and Amsterdam. Cell phones banned, soft lighting, personalized recommendations from our "literary pharmacy." An oasis of culture and sociality where, between pages, new friendships are born united by a passion for reading.

170+ Participants
Book Fair Turin
04 Putignano

Carnevale di Putignano

Andrea is the philosophical director of Europe's oldest Carnival. He chose the theme of Subversion for the 2024 float parades and Paradox for 2025. A work focused on recovering the philosophical roots of Carnival and updating the themes of the papier-mâché masters.

Philosophical direction · Andrea Colamedici

05 Bari

BariDiversa – Festival of Queer Thinking

A provocative invitation to recognize and honor the diversity that inhabits our city and each of us, to not be afraid of being ourselves. Thinking in a queer way means striving to overcome binaries and labels, embracing Emily Dickinson's teaching: "Tell all the truth but tell it slant."

Organized for the City of Bari

06 European Parliament

Pensare Europeo

A series of encounters created in collaboration with the European Parliament, designed to strengthen the sense of belonging to the European community by connecting MEPs with philosophers, activists, historians and artists. A bridge between institutions and citizens through cultural dialogue.

Podcast and live events

07 Milan · Rome

Festa della Filosofia

Living philosophy as the ancients did: Socrates met students and friends in convivial settings, Plato's books are conversations, Aristotle "walked" with his disciples. Not a traditional conference with frontal lectures, but an event with physical and mental exercises, philosophical walks and conversations.

Triennale Milan
Monk Rome
08 Rome · Villa Ada

Tlon Fest – Thinking Music

Three days of music and thought. Philosophers, musicians, artists and intellectuals unite high and low, serious and playful, moving from Mozart to Raffaella Carrà, from the philosophy of Grunge to Franco Battiato, from '90s boy bands to AI in music. An intellectual roller coaster concluding with a literary party.

3 Days
Free Entry
09 International Streaming

Prendiamola con Filosofia

After the first edition that reached two million viewers during lockdown, gathered with the intent of using philosophy as a tool to face the pandemic moment, it returned in an international guise. From 11 am until 11 pm, a relay with big names from the global cultural scene.

2M Viewers

10 Rome · AANT

GenIALAB

Transdisciplinary research hub of the Academy of Arts and New Technologies. A permanent interdisciplinary observatory, open to dialogue with external experts, to co-create innovative projects. A laboratory of excellence uniting the Italian tradition of art, philosophy and communication with technological innovation.

Andrea Colamedici · President
