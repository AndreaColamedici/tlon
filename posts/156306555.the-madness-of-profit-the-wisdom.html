This conversation took place on January 24, 2025 with Fritjof Capra, the author of the legendary The Tao of Physics (Adelphi, 1982). During the interview, fundamental reflections emerge on the relationship between living systems, global crises and the future of society. Capra, known for his interdisciplinary approach that intertwines science, philosophy and sustainability, offers a unique perspective on how systems thinking can help us face (or better said, navigate, as we will see) the complex challenges of our time.
